Make room for better sleep

Measure your bed frame and room before choosing a mattress. Allow 24–30 inches of walking space on the sides you use.

  • Twin & Twin XL38 inches wide. Twin XL adds 5 inches in length.
  • Full & Queen54 and 60 inches wide. Queen is 5 inches longer than Full.
  • King & Cal KingKing offers more width; Cal King offers 4 inches of extra length.
  • Split KingTwo Twin XL mattresses side by side, each 38 × 80 inches.
  • Split HeadA split at the head end. Check compatibility with your adjustable base.
